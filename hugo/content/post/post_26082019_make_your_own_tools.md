# Make your own tools

Don't be afraid to create your own tools.
If you need a specific tool for a specific job, don't be afraid to create a new tool even if you have to reinvent the wheel.

You'll come out of it with a better understanding of the problem and hopefully with something customizable to your needs.
Hey, maybe some other person will find it useful.

That's it.
